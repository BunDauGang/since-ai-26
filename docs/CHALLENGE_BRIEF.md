# Official Challenge Brief — Lindström (Since AI Hackathon 2026)

## 1. Challenge Title
**Customer Value & Sustainability Review Generator**

## 2. Guiding Question
> *How might we use AI and transparent calculation logic to turn multi-source, multi-site customer data into a dynamic Customer Value & Sustainability Review that shows value created, highlights customer- and industry-relevant priorities, and recommends practical next actions for annual reviews and strategic account discussions?*

---

## 3. Four-Stage Build Flow (Three-Day Prototype)
1. **INPUT:** Customer, reporting period, agenda/priority, and comparison baseline.
2. **CONSOLIDATE:** Multi-site data with customer roll-up and site/product drill-down.
3. **GENERATE:** Value review, AI/rules observations, and recommended actions.
4. **EXPORT:** Interactive dashboard plus customer-ready PowerPoint/PDF.

> **SUCCESS =** the prototype communicates customer value and creates a better strategic discussion.

---

## 4. Evaluation Criteria & Weights

| # | Criterion | Weight | What Judges Look For |
| :- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | **Customer & Business Value** | **25%** | Makes value created visible; supports a credible annual review or steering-committee discussion; shows what happened, what matters for this customer, and what action to consider next. |
| 2 | **Feasibility & Completeness** | **20%** | Working interactive dashboard + customer-ready PowerPoint/PDF export demonstrating an end-to-end workflow, including customer-level aggregation and site drill-down. |
| 3 | **Sustainability Credibility & Transparency** | **20%** | Baseline choice, boundaries, sources, assumptions, confidence, synthetic/estimated status, and limitations are visible. Footprint and handprint claims remain understandable and defensible. |
| 4 | **Data Handling & Calculation Logic** | **15%** | Coherently combines supplied sources, handles missing/unmapped values, and produces reproducible calculations or clearly marked estimates. Repair, laundry, logistics, and circularity logic is traceable. |
| 5 | **Dynamic Usability & Communication Quality** | **10%** | Sales/KAM and customer users can tailor the view by priority or prompt. Emphasizes relevant customer and industry needs without requiring sustainability expertise. |
| 6 | **Scalability & Maintainability** | **10%** | Shows a credible path to approved real data, larger multi-site customers, additional industries/countries/service lines, and a future eLindström customer decision-maker dashboard. |

> **Judging Note:** Teams are **not** rewarded for the highest claimed environmental or financial savings. Judges prefer transparent logic, relevant customer value, actionable observations, good uncertainty handling, and a credible path to production.

---

## 5. Required Submission Deliverables

- [ ] **Working interactive dashboard prototype** for a fictional multi-site customer (`NorthStar Foods Group`, 24 sites).
- [ ] **Customer-ready PowerPoint or PDF** annual/steering-committee review.
- [ ] **Demonstration of prompt- or priority-driven personalization** for at least **two** customer priorities (e.g., water, cost, emissions, circularity, or service performance).
- [ ] **At least three AI- or rules-generated observations/recommendations** explaining what the customer should discuss or improve next.
- [ ] **Short final pitch/demo** explaining the customer problem, workflow, business value, and key design choices.
- [ ] **Brief methodology note** covering selected baselines, data sources, footprint/handprint logic, assumptions, uncertainty, missing data, and limitations.
- [ ] **Short data-readiness note** mapping prototype fields to potential future Lindström source types and identifying key gaps (customer hierarchy, site mapping, unavailable measures).
- [ ] **Source code repository** with basic run instructions.
- [ ] **Strict data labeling:** Clearly distinguish synthetic/estimated values from public-reference information and never present outputs as audited or verified Lindström/customer claims.
