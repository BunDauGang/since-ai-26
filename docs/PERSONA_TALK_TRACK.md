# Customer Review Persona Talk Track — NorthStar Foods Group

## Important data-use note

This talk track is written for the NorthStar Foods Group demonstration scenario. The repository states that customer, site, operational, baseline and factor figures are **synthetic and illustrative** unless explicitly marked `PUBLIC_REFERENCE`. Therefore, the figures below must be described as demo outputs—not as verified customer results, audited sustainability disclosures, guaranteed savings or actual financial benefits. Before any external customer use, replace them with validated data and approved calculation factors.

### Illustrative what-if scenarios

- **Garment reclaim:** reclaiming 50% of the idle garments at the five lowest-use sites represents approximately 719 garments and could increase the allocation-use indicator from 83.5% to approximately 85.5%.
- **Repair improvement:** closing half of the gap to the site repair-rate benchmark represents approximately 265 fewer repairs in the illustrative scenario.
- **Idle stock value:** approximately €223,315 is an illustrative KAM-only estimate; the 719-garment reclaim scenario corresponds to approximately €30,700 of potential stock value. These are not confirmed cash savings or realized financial benefits.

> Definition reminder: the 83.5% allocation-use indicator and the count of garments not seen in circulation are different measures. Explain the denominator and calculation definition shown in the application before presenting the percentage.

## 1. CEO — Business value and strategic partnership

**Opening**

“Our goal today is to demonstrate the overall value your organization can receive from its textile service partnership with Lindström and identify opportunities for long-term improvement.”

**Key messages**

1. “The demonstration covers 24 sites across Finland, Sweden and Poland, at least 9,607 wearers and approximately 502 tonnes of textiles washed.”
2. “The sample service data shows an on-time delivery rate of 98.3%, representing 11,181 on-time cycles out of 11,371.”
3. “In an illustrative garment-reclaim scenario, recovering half of the idle garments at the five lowest-use sites would bring approximately 719 garments back into circulation and raise the allocation-use indicator from 83.5% to around 85.5%.”

**Question to ask**

“Which strategic priority should we focus on together to create the greatest value for your organization in the coming year?”

**Transition**

“Once we agree on the strategic priority, we can translate it into measurable operational actions and a review plan.”

## 2. CFO — Financial value and cost transparency

**Opening**

“We want to make the financial implications of your textile service more transparent and help you compare service models using clearly stated assumptions.”

**Key messages**

1. “The model estimates approximately 1.4 million litres of water saving compared with the local-laundry baseline. The scenario range is approximately 0.45–2.36 million litres.”
2. “The corresponding estimated emissions difference is approximately 255 tonnes of CO₂e, with a scenario range of approximately 189–321 tonnes.”
3. “The illustrative idle-stock estimate is approximately €223,315. Reclaiming 719 garments corresponds to around €30,700 in potential stock value in this scenario. This is not a confirmed cash saving.”

**Question to ask**

“Which financial assumptions and cost areas should we validate first to identify the most realistic opportunities?”

**Transition**

“We can then test the agreed assumptions and show how the financial picture changes under low, base and high scenarios.”

## Presenter guardrails

- Say **“the model estimates”**, **“the demonstration data shows”** or **“in this illustrative scenario”**.
- Do not say **“verified savings”**, **“audited results”** or **“NorthStar achieved”** unless validated source data supports that statement.
- Keep the local-laundry baseline visible whenever presenting water or emissions comparisons.
- Explain the scenario range and assumptions; do not present the base case as a guaranteed outcome.
- Treat the €223,315 and €30,700 figures as illustrative stock-value estimates, not realized savings.
- If asked whether the figures are real, answer: **“No. These are synthetic demonstration figures used to show how the review workflow works. A live customer review would use validated operational data and approved calculation factors.”**
